# Wake For Real Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

Wake For Real is provided by **Asuzsoftware**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Wake For Real mobile application.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Wake For Real may process limited information required to operate the app, including:

- account information such as your email address and account identifier when you sign in;
- authentication information provided by the sign-in provider;
- subscription and purchase status required to determine access to Premium features;
- alarm settings, preferences, and app configuration;
- limited technical information required for authentication, security, purchase verification, and app functionality.

Alarm settings and normal application preferences may be stored locally on your device.

## 2. Authentication

Wake For Real uses Firebase Authentication for account sign-in functionality.

Depending on the sign-in method you choose, Google, Apple, Firebase, or another supported authentication provider may process information such as your email address, account identifier, and authentication credentials.

Wake For Real does not receive or store your Apple, Google, or other account password.

## 3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Wake For Real may offer in-app purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions.

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Asuzsoftware does not receive or store your full payment card information.

Purchase and subscription information may be processed to:

- activate Premium access;
- determine whether a subscription is active;
- restore eligible purchases;
- manage subscription entitlement status.

Subscription billing, cancellations, refunds, and payment processing are subject to the rules and systems of the store through which the purchase was made.

## 4. Alarm and App Data

Alarm times, alarm configuration, wake-up settings, preferences, and similar application data may be stored locally on your device.

Wake For Real does not require this information to be publicly shared with other users.

## 5. Analytics, Advertising and Session Replay

Wake For Real does not currently use session replay technology to record users' screens or keystrokes.

Asuzsoftware does not sell personal information.

Wake For Real does not use personal information for targeted advertising unless this Privacy Policy is updated and any consent required by applicable law is obtained.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Wake For Real may rely on third-party services necessary for app functionality, authentication, purchases, and distribution, including:

- Google and Firebase;
- Apple and the Apple App Store;
- Google Play;
- subscription or purchase-management services if enabled.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

## 7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Wake For Real, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and maintain transaction records where required.

Information stored only on your device may be removed when you delete the application or clear its local data.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion

If you have a Wake For Real account, you may use any account-deletion functionality made available in the application or contact us to request deletion of eligible account information.

Some information may be retained where required by law, for fraud prevention, security purposes, or financial and transaction recordkeeping.

For account or data deletion requests, contact:

**asuzsoftware@gmail.com**

## 9. Children's Privacy

Wake For Real is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information processed through Wake For Real.

However, no electronic system, device, or method of data transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 11. International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live, subject to applicable data-protection requirements.

## 12. Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of certain processing.

You may contact us to make a privacy-related request.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Wake For Real, our services, third-party integrations, store requirements, or applicable laws change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

## 14. Contact
