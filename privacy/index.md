# Alarm Asuz Pro Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

Alarm Asuz Pro is provided by **Asuzsoftware**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Alarm Asuz Pro mobile application.

## 1. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Alarm Asuz Pro may process limited information required for the app to function, including:

- account information such as your email address and account identifier when you sign in;
- authentication information provided by your selected sign-in provider;
- subscription and purchase status required to determine access to Premium features;
- alarm settings, alarm times, wake-up missions, preferences, and application configuration;
- sleep-session information such as sleep start time, wake time, total sleep duration, sleep goals, and sleep history;
- limited device motion sensor information used for features such as the Shake Challenge;
- limited technical information required for authentication, security, purchase verification, alarm reliability, and application functionality.

Alarm settings, sleep records, mission progress, goals, and normal application preferences may be stored locally on your device.

## 2. Authentication

Alarm Asuz Pro may use Firebase Authentication and supported sign-in providers for account authentication.

Depending on the sign-in method you choose, Google, Apple, Firebase, or another supported authentication provider may process information such as your email address, account identifier, and authentication credentials.

Alarm Asuz Pro does not receive or store your Apple, Google, or other account password.

## 3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Alarm Asuz Pro may offer Premium features through in-app purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions.

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Asuzsoftware does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Purchase and subscription information may be processed to:

- activate Premium access;
- determine whether a subscription is active;
- restore eligible purchases;
- verify subscription entitlement status;
- provide access to purchased features.

Subscription billing, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and payment processing are subject to the rules and systems of the store through which the purchase was made.

## 4. Alarm and Reliability Permissions

Alarm Asuz Pro may request device permissions or direct you to device settings that are necessary for reliable alarm functionality.

Depending on your device and operating-system version, these may include:

- notification permission;
- exact alarm capability;
- full-screen alarm functionality;
- battery optimization or unrestricted battery-use settings;
- foreground-service functionality required for active alarms;
- vibration and wake-related functionality.

These permissions and settings are used to help alarms operate reliably.

Alarm Asuz Pro does not use these permissions to access unrelated personal information.

Some Android manufacturers may apply additional battery or background restrictions. The app may provide instructions or shortcuts to relevant system settings where appropriate.

## 5. Sleep Tracking

Alarm Asuz Pro may provide a sleep-duration and sleep-routine tracking feature.

When you use this feature, the app may process and store:

- sleep start time;
- wake time;
- total sleep duration;
- sleep-duration goals;
- previous sleep sessions;
- routine and consistency information calculated from those sessions.

Sleep information is intended to be stored locally on your device unless a separate synchronization feature is clearly introduced and disclosed in the future.

Alarm Asuz Pro does not claim to medically detect, measure, or diagnose:

- REM sleep;
- deep sleep;
- light sleep;
- sleep apnea;
- sleep disorders;
- heart rate;
- heart conditions;
- any other medical condition.

The sleep feature is intended for general routine and duration tracking and is not a medical device or medical service.

## 6. Motion Sensor Information

Certain wake-up missions, such as Shake Challenge, may use your device's motion or accelerometer sensors.

Motion information is used to detect the required movement for the mission.

Alarm Asuz Pro does not use this feature to determine your location.

Raw motion sensor readings are not intended to be uploaded or sold and are used only as necessary to operate the relevant mission.

## 7. Alarm Missions

Alarm Asuz Pro may offer wake-up missions such as mathematical challenges, memory challenges, movement-based challenges, typing tasks, games, or other alarm-dismissal activities.

Information needed to complete these missions may be processed locally on your device.

Mission information is used to operate the alarm and determine whether the selected wake-up task has been completed.

## 8. Analytics, Advertising and Session Replay

Alarm Asuz Pro does not currently use session-replay technology to record users' screens or keystrokes.

Asuzsoftware does not sell personal information.

Alarm Asuz Pro does not use personal information for targeted advertising unless this Privacy Policy is updated and any consent required by applicable law is obtained.

If analytics or advertising technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and applicable store privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

## 9. Third-Party Services

Alarm Asuz Pro may rely on third-party services necessary for authentication, purchases, distribution, and application functionality, including:

- Google and Firebase;
- Apple and the Apple App Store;
- Google Play;
- subscription or purchase-management providers if enabled.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and legal obligations.

## 10. Local Data

Certain information may be stored locally on your device, including:

- alarm configuration;
- alarm preferences;
- selected wake-up missions;
- sleep history;
- sleep goals;
- application preferences;
- feature state required for normal operation.

Locally stored information may be removed when you clear the application's data or delete the application, subject to your device and operating-system behavior.

## 11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Alarm Asuz Pro, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, maintain security, resolve disputes, and maintain transaction records where legally required.

Information stored only on your device remains subject to your device's storage and deletion behavior.
